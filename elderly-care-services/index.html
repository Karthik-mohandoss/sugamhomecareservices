Types of Assistance Available

A clear breakdown of duties and care activities covered under our Dignified Elderly Care Services at Home.

✓

Fall Prevention & Walking Support

Steadying hands during walking, navigating stairs, using walking aids, and securing bathroom visits.

✓

Bathing, Toileting & Grooming

Patient, respectful assistance with personal hygiene, sponge/shower baths, oral care, and clean clothes.

✓

Medication Timing Reminders

Ensuring seniors take their daily cardiac, diabetic, or general health tablets strictly on schedule.

✓

Nutritious Senior Meal Assistance

Serving warm, easily digestible meals, monitoring fluid intake, and helping with feeding if required.

✓

Companionship & Cognitive Engagement

Meaningful conversations, reading devotional books or newspapers, and sharing stories to uplift spirits.

✓

Daily Health Observation

Noting daily mood, sleep quality, appetite, and alerting family members to any noticeable changes.
