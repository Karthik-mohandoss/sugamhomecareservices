Service Selection Guide

Which Service Fits Your Family's Needs?

A quick comparison to help you choose the right level of support.

🧹

Household Help

Maid Services: Best when you need hands for daily floor cleaning, kitchen dishes, and organizing without personal care duties.

🤝

Personal Caregiving

Caretaker / Elderly Care: Best for elders or recovering family members needing daily physical mobility, feeding, hygiene, and companionship.

👩‍⚕️

Clinical Nursing

Nursing Services at Home: Best when a doctor has prescribed specific medical procedures such as injections, IV fluids, or sterile wound dressings.
