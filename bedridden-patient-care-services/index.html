Types of Assistance Available

A clear breakdown of duties and care activities covered under our Bedridden Patient Care Services.

✓

2-Hour Position Turning

Strict scheduled body repositioning between left lateral, back, and right lateral to prevent pressure ulcers.

✓

Bedsore Prevention & Skin Care

Inspecting pressure points (sacrum, heels, elbows), applying barrier creams, and ensuring wrinkle-free linen.

✓

Daily Sponge Bathing in Bed

Warm, gentle head-to-toe sponge bathing, hair cleaning, mouth hygiene, and skin moisturizing in bed.

✓

Adult Diaper & Incontinence Hygiene

Frequent diaper inspections, prompt cleaning with wet wipes, and maintaining sterile perineal hygiene.

✓

Catheter & Drainage Bag Care

Emptying urinary drainage bags hygienically, monitoring output volume, and preventing tube entanglement.

✓

Assisted Feeding & Hydration

Careful elevated feeding of pureed diets, soups, or assisting family with Ryle's tube feeding timings.
